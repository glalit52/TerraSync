What this is. TerraShield is an analyst-assistance and monitoring system. It reports what changed, where, when, and how far that departs from a site's own recorded history. It makes no assessment of cause, ownership or intent, and every finding is subject to analyst review.

Findings on this page were produced by the pipeline in src/terrashield/ from modelled imagery over four real civilian sites. Detection precision, recall and confidence calibration are measured, not claimed — run terrashield evaluate.
